# The Witcher 3 artwork

The four wallpapers are official The Witcher 3: Wild Hunt art by CD PROJEKT RED, shared here
as fan content for personal desktops. CD PROJEKT S.A. owns them; this theme is not affiliated
with or endorsed by CD PROJEKT.

- `01-complete-edition.jpg`: Geralt in the mountains, Complete Edition key art
- `02-griffin-hunt.jpg`: Geralt fighting a griffin
- `03-ciri.jpg`: Ciri by the campfire
- `04-tenth-anniversary.jpg`: Geralt, Ciri and the Wild Hunt, tenth anniversary art

The palette is drawn from these pictures: night slate background, parchment text, the wolf
medallion's gold as accent, fire red, forest green, steel blue, violet and Ciri's ash
teal.
